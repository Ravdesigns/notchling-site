What it does all day

Pick what lives up there

🗂️

It holds your things in its mouth

Drag a file, a link, a line of text, or a screenshot straight off the floating thumbnail. The mouth opens, it swallows, and it hands everything back whenever you want it. Then right-click a held image and it will chew: to JPEG, to PNG, smaller, or read the words out of a screenshot for you. All on your own Mac. Those last few are what other notch apps charge ten to thirty dollars for.

🐣

A someone, not a something

It watches your cursor, blinks, and naps when you do. Drag it sideways to reach whatever fell behind it; it drifts home on its own.

🔋

It feels how your Mac feels

Plugged in, its cheeks flush warm. Battery low, it yawns. Camera on, it shuts its eyes. Caps Lock, offline, a stuffed disk. It notices, and never says a word.

⛅

Your sky, small

A breathing sun, a cratered moon, rain that splashes on the lip, forked lightning. Right-click to switch, or to hide it down to a bare notch.

🤖

It watches your agents

Running Claude Code? An ember pulse while your agents work, and when one gets stuck waiting on you it glances down. Click, and you are in that terminal.
